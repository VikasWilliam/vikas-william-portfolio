Privacy Policy

Pocket FX: Currency Converter

Developer: Vikas William
Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how Pocket FX handles information when you use the Android app. Pocket FX provides currency conversions using reference exchange rates.

1. Information handled by the app

Pocket FX does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or payment information. Conversion amounts are processed on your device and are not included in requests to the exchange-rate provider.

The current version does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not operate a server that stores your conversion amounts or a personal user profile.

2. Exchange-rate requests

The app connects to the Frankfurter API over HTTPS to retrieve exchange rates. Requests include the selected currency codes, such as USD and INR.

To deliver a response, the API provider and its hosting infrastructure receive your IP address and standard network request information. Their handling and retention of server logs are controlled by those providers, not by Pocket FX. We do not send your entered conversion amount, name, email, or account credentials to this API.

3. Data stored on your device

Pocket FX stores previously retrieved exchange rates, currency pairs, rate dates, and retrieval timestamps in the app's local storage. This allows previously cached rates to be used when a network request is unavailable.

Cached records may remain until replaced or until you clear the app's storage. You can remove local app data through Android Settings by selecting Pocket FX and choosing Clear storage or Clear data. The exact wording depends on your device.

4. Permissions and security

Internet access is used to request exchange rates. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location for currency conversion.

Exchange-rate requests use HTTPS to protect information in transit. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. Network information is transmitted to the exchange-rate provider as described above to operate the currency conversion service.

6. Support communications and deletion

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and retain it only as long as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations.

You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us below. Pocket FX does not create user accounts, so there is no in-app account to delete. Local cached data can be removed using the device settings described above.

7. Children's privacy

Pocket FX does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support communications, contact us so we can address the request and delete that information as appropriate.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
